# Hi, I'm Ruven

I'm based in Singapore and work with enterprise data and AI systems. I'm interested in what happens between a promising demo and something people can actually use, particularly when it comes to integration, data access, security and evaluation.

I use GitHub to try things out and document what I learn along the way. Some projects are further along than others.

## A few projects

- [AI Assurance Workbench](https://github.com/RuvenGuna94/AI-assurance-workbench) — Testing a local LLM application with ASQI Engineer and Garak, and documenting the findings, limitations and supporting evidence.
- [Property Operations RAG Workbench](https://github.com/RuvenGuna94/property-ops-rag-workbench) — Bringing documents and structured asset records together to answer operational questions, while working through citations, access boundaries and tracing.
- [Experiments with AI Agents](https://github.com/RuvenGuna94/Experimenting-With-AI-Agents) — Exploring agent collaboration and task orchestration with CrewAI.
- [Dialogue Summarization with PEFT](https://github.com/RuvenGuna94/Dialogue-Summary-PEFT-Fine-Tuning) — Comparing full and parameter-efficient fine-tuning for a summarisation task.

## Outside of work

I spend some of my free time volunteering, from academic befriending to helping run community AI education sessions. We introduce people to AI, explore practical ways to use it, and make the technology a little less intimidating.

If you'd like to learn more about what we do or join me at an upcoming volunteer session, feel free to [reach out on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ruven-guna/).
